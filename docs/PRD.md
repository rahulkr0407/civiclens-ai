# CivicLens AI - Product Requirements Document

## Vision

To empower every citizen with clear, neutral, and evidence-based understanding of public issues, laws, policies, and current events through AI.

---

## Problem

Today's information ecosystem has several challenges:

- Information is scattered across many platforms.
- Context is often missing.
- Many explanations are difficult for beginners.
- Different age groups require different levels of explanation.
- Users struggle to distinguish verified facts from opinions.

---

## Solution

CivicLens AI provides personalized explanations based on the user's:

- Age
- Education level
- Language
- Knowledge level

Every topic includes:

- Summary
- Timeline
- Background
- Why it matters
- Current status
- Multiple perspectives
- Official sources
- AI Q&A

---

## Target Users

### Primary

- School students
- College students
- Young professionals
- First-time voters

### Secondary

- Teachers
- UPSC aspirants
- Parents
- Researchers

---

## MVP Features

- Landing Page
- Authentication
- User Profile
- Search
- Topic Pages
- AI Chat
- Bill Tracker
- Protest Tracker
- Dashboard

---

## Core Principles

1. Neutral and evidence-based.
2. Cite reliable sources.
3. Separate facts from opinions.
4. Explain according to the user's level.
5. Update information regularly.
