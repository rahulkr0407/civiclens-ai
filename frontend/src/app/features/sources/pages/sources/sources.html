Why CivicLens lists sources

CivicLens is a neutral, educational explainer for Indian civic topics. We are not the original author of these documents — we help you understand them. Sources are the references we use to prepare each topic.

We prefer authoritative references such as official government bodies, legislative records, and reputed research organisations. Listing a source here means we used it for reference — it does not mean CivicLens endorses its views, and it is never a statement of our political position.

Always read the original source for the full and official context.
