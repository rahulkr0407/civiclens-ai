What is CivicLens?

CivicLens AI is an educational platform that helps citizens understand Indian civic topics — laws, policies, government decisions, elections and public issues — through simple, neutral and well-structured explanations.

In a noisy information environment, CivicLens exists so that anyone — a school student discovering a topic for the first time, a young professional, or a first-time voter — can build a solid, balanced foundation before forming an opinion.

Neutrality and multiple viewpoints

Complex issues rarely have a single "correct" answer. CivicLens presents each topic with the viewpoints of different groups — supporters, critics, and everyone in between — so you can weigh the arguments yourself.

We work hard to stay fact-based and neutral, and we separate established facts from opinions.

🎓

Personalised by age and education level

The same topic can be explained very differently to a Class 8 student and to a graduate preparing for exams. CivicLens adapts explanations to your age, education level and interests — so you learn at your own pace, without being talked down to or overwhelmed.

📚

Why reliable sources matter

Trustworthy understanding begins with trustworthy references. We base our topics on authoritative sources — official government bodies, legislative records and reputed research organisations — and list them openly so you can always check the original material. Sources are references, not endorsements.
