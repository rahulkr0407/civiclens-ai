Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, age, education level and topic interests. This "learning profile" lets CivicLens explain topics at a level that suits you.

2. How we use it

Your learning profile is used to personalise AI explanations and recommendations. Your saved topics, saved explanations and chat history are stored to power your personal dashboard.

3. AI processing

When you ask for an AI explanation, your learning profile and the topic you requested are sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to generate a tailored response.

4. Passwords & security

Passwords are stored only as secure one-way hashes. Session tokens are stored in your browser (localStorage/sessionStorage) and can be cleared by logging out.

5. No selling of data

We never sell your personal data. It is used solely to operate and improve CivicLens AI.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete items from your dashboard at any time. For a full account deletion, contact the CivicLens AI team and we will remove your account and associated data.

7. Contact

For any privacy questions, reach out through the about or sources pages.
